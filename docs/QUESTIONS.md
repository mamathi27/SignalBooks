# 10 Real Questions My User Will Ask

These questions represent research tasks that SignalBooks should answer using telecom and network-equipment company reports. The expected source locations below are targets for retrieval, not verified citations. Before evaluation, confirm the relevant reporting periods, figures, and page references in the collected documents.

| # | Question | Expected source documents | Needs more than one page? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What was Bharti Airtel's India mobile ARPU in its latest available reported quarter, and for which quarter was it reported? | Airtel quarterly results and investor presentations | No, if one page contains the figure and reporting period |
| 2 | How has Bharti Airtel's India mobile ARPU changed across the last three completed financial years? | Airtel annual reports and quarterly results | Yes |
| 3 | How much did Bharti Airtel spend on capital expenditure in its latest completed financial year, and what reasons did management give for that spending? | Airtel annual report, financial statements, and management discussion | Yes |
| 4 | What progress has Bharti Airtel reported in expanding its 5G network, and which coverage or deployment metrics does it disclose? | Airtel annual reports and quarterly investor presentations | Yes |
| 5 | What order book has Tejas Networks reported in its latest available results, and how has it changed across recent reported quarters? | Tejas Networks quarterly results presentations and annual reports | Yes |
| 6 | Which major customers, markets, and product categories does Tejas Networks identify in its reports? | Tejas Networks annual reports and investor presentations | Yes |
| 7 | What tenancy ratio has Indus Towers reported in its latest available results, and how has it changed across the last three completed financial years? | Indus Towers annual reports and quarterly results | Yes |
| 8 | How have Vodafone Idea's revenue, losses, and disclosed debt-related obligations changed across the last three completed financial years? | Vodafone Idea annual reports and quarterly results | Yes |
| 9 | Which business segments contribute to Tata Communications' revenue, and how has each segment's contribution changed across recent reporting periods? | Tata Communications annual reports and quarterly results | Yes |
| 10 | What key risks and growth opportunities do telecom operators and network-equipment companies identify, and how do their disclosures compare? | Annual reports, risk-factor sections, management discussion, and investor presentations from the companies in scope | Yes |

## Evaluation notes

- Record the exact document filename and PDF page number(s) supporting each answer.
- Include the reporting period and units for every financial figure.
- For comparisons, use consistent financial years or quarters and explain any differences in reporting definitions.
- If the available documents do not contain enough evidence, the system should say so rather than invent an answer.
- Score answer correctness, source relevance, page-level citation accuracy, and whether the answer acknowledges missing evidence.
